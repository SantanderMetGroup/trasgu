Ship-wake execution environment
===============================

System: Altamira HPC infrastructure, University of Cantabria / IFCA-CSIC
Scheduler: SLURM, orchestrated by Snakemake
Initial phase: compute partition, 8 worker processes per chunk
Resumed phase: wncompute_ifca partition, 32 worker processes per chunk
Chunk size: 4,000,000 matrices
Number of chunks: 166

The exact infrastructure-specific configuration files are included under
`workflow_snapshot`. Package versions not printed in the historical logs were
not reconstructed.
